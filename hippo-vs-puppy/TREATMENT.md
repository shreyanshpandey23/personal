# Whose Cone? — treatment

Style: Color-Coded Mechanism (`precise-mechanism`). 10 s, 1080×1920 vertical, 24 fps. No voice, no subtitles (the user chose music + effects); English on-screen labels only.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Fault hunt** — the bench "machine" jams; remove systems (hippo, puppy) one by one to find what is stopping the cone. Too slow for 10 s.
2. **Parameter sweep** — a tug-force slider in the accent swings from hippo to puppy while the cone slides. Clever, but the fight loses its characters.
3. **Follow the force, then one slowed contact** (chosen) — the tug is shown as a running mechanism: two pulling systems and a prize; force alternates on the beat until the scoop lets go in true slow motion and lands between them, so the "machine" settles at 50 : 50. It has a subject (the cone), a turn (the scoop pops), and an ending different from the start (shared, not owned).

Opening: a single part in the accent — the cherry — then a fast pull-back to find where it lives (not the demo's watch-detail pull-back: we start on a grey, undyed bench and the colours flow outward from the cone). Ending: a locked overhead verdict frame, the number held. Score: marimba + glockenspiel + pizzicato-style pluck bass at 120 bpm, tug creaks on every beat, one silence before the pop.

## Logline and arc
A blue baby hippo and a small puppy tug one ice-cream cone across a park bench until the scoop flies off and lands between them — and they share it.
Setup (0–2.5 s) → tug (2.5–5.5 s) → turn: the scoop lets go, slowed (5.5–7.4 s) → ending: shared, 50 : 50 (7.4–10 s).

## Systems and colour (one hue per system)
- ground `#F3F0EA`; neutral (bench, empty cone) `#DDD8CF`; ink `#25282D`
- **warm yellow `#E6B94F` = the puppy** (the challenger, energy coming in)
- **grey-green `#79B09F` = the ice-cream scoop** (mint; the prize the force passes through)
- **grey-violet `#8A97D4` = the hippo** (the owner, holding control) — close to the plush's soft blue
- **accent `#E8472A` = the cherry**, only at its moments (the opening detail and the slowed pop)

# Board brief: FreshFold Laundry

(This brief stands on its own. The board has not spoken to the founder.)

## The founder's pitch, verbatim
> FreshFold Laundry is a new laundry service in Lucknow for people who don't have the time or energy to deal with washing clothes. We charge by weight, so you don't have to count items or worry about confusing bills. Bring your clothes in, we weigh them, and you pay a fixed rate per kg: ₹50 per kg for wash and fold, or ₹60 per kg for wash and iron. Your clothes are sorted by colour and fabric, washed with good-quality detergents, dried properly, and then neatly folded or ironed, whichever you choose. We also offer same-day service: drop your clothes off in the morning and pick them up fresh and ready by the evening, so you never have to wait days for a favourite shirt or your office wear. We're a good fit for working professionals, students in hostels and PGs, bachelors, and busy families who want a laundry partner they can rely on every week. FreshFold Laundry is about three simple things: fair and clear pricing, quick turnaround, and clothes you'll be happy to wear. Hand us your laundry and get your time back.

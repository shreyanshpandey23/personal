# Product lens
Score: 5 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Pricing is simple.** One number per kg, no item counting. That kind of simplicity is hard to get right, and it is already in the offer.
- **The pitch already has a one-sentence core:** "Hand us your laundry and get your time back." That is a promise a customer can feel.
- **Same-day turnaround is a concrete experience.** "Drop off in the morning, wear it tonight" describes the moment the customer uses the service, not a feature list.
- **The pitch cares about craft:** sorting by colour and fabric, good detergents, proper drying. This is where quality lives in laundry, and most customers never see it.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The experience contradicts the promise.** The pitch says "get your time back", but the only channel is walk-in drop-off and pick-up, which means two trips a day. The customer's first five minutes are spent getting to a shop. That is a seam right at the core of the offer. Can the target customer reach the shop easily? That is unknown.
2. **No focus.** Four customer groups (professionals, hostel/PG students, bachelors, families) have different volumes, schedules, budgets and fabrics. A product built for all four is built brilliantly for none. Nothing has been cut.
3. **Same-day breaks quietly at volume.** It is unknown whether daily capacity and per-kg cost can carry same-day service on a busy morning. If the promise slips, or ironing gets rushed to hit it, the brand breaks exactly where it is meant to stand out. How damage, lost items and mix-ups are handled is not addressed.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is FreshFold in one sentence, and who is the *one* first customer?
- Walk me through a customer's day: where they live, when they drop off, when they pick up. Does it really save them time?
- What is the daily kg capacity at which same-day still holds? (Answered by /founder-ops and /founder-cfo.)
- What is the real cost per kg, including ironing labour? (/founder-cfo)
- What do local dhobis and app-based pickup services charge and promise? (/founder-competitors)
- Would your chosen customer pay for pickup/delivery, or switch for same-day alone? (/founder-consumer)
- What happens when a shirt is lost, shrunk or stained? Who tells the customer, and how?
- What does the customer see at the counter, on the receipt, and in the "ready" message?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Choose one first customer group and one neighbourhood dense with that group. Cut the rest for now.
2. Make the trip match the promise: put the shop on the customer's daily route, or test pickup/delivery for that group.
3. Run a capacity test proving same-day holds at target volume without lower quality, with numbers from /founder-ops and /founder-cfo.
4. Write down the full touchpoint flow (drop-off, tagging, the "ready" notice, pick-up, complaints) and a damage/loss policy before opening.

# Bowlista

- What it is: a 100% pure-veg, plant-powered quick-service outlet selling rice bowls, burritos/wraps, subs/sandwiches and cold brew, with a bold, fresh, fun brand.
- Who it is for: college students in Lucknow, specifically students of BBD (Babu Banarasi Das) University / campus.
- What it sells, at what price: entry "mini" range at ₹69 each — mini rice bowl, mini burrito, mini sandwich. Full-size bowls, wraps, subs and cold brew also on the menu; their prices are not set yet (open question).
- Where and how: one outlet in Crown Mall, near the BBD campus, Lucknow. Dine-in / takeaway at the outlet, plus delivery via Swiggy and Zomato. Branded packaging (bowls, foil-wrapped burritos, sub boxes, cold brew cups, kraft bags), uniforms and signage are already designed.

# Store in a Box — Bowlista counter explainer — treatment

Style: Isometric People Scene (`isometric-picture-book`). ~32 s, 1080×1920 vertical (Reels), 30 fps. English narration (Kokoro, offline), one subtitle line per narration line. Audience: customers. Facts: only what the brand boards show (menu categories, "100% Pure Veg", pickup); no invented numbers. Logo redrawn in code from the brand board (no file supplied). Third-party delivery brands are not named or drawn.

## Three candidate structures
1. **A day in one place as five small rules** (open, rush, lull, close…) — too long for a Reel, and it is not what makes Bowlista different.
2. **Two ways compared** (a messy food-court counter vs Bowlista's line) — needs a rival to look bad; off-brand ("Bold. Fresh. Fun.").
3. **One thing travelling through several stops** (chosen) — one bowl travels the length of the counter, one stop per beat: order → base → fresh fillings → cold brew → pickup. The customer walks beside it, up-right, seen from behind; the order of the stops *is* the explanation of how the store works. Bookended by the "store in a box": the kiosk arrives as a closed blue box whose lid flips open.

Opening: **lid off** — a closed cobalt kiosk box, lid hinged on the far edge, flips paper-side-up and glides away; the camera rises into the counter; "STORE IN A BOX" lands on the floor. Ending: the customer walks off up-right with the bag; scale jump out to the whole kiosk, "PLANT-POWERED" left painted on the floor.

## Logline and arc
A whole Bowlista counter unpacks from one box, and a single order shows how it works, stop by stop, until the bowl lands on the pickup shelf.
Setup (box opens) → journey (five stops) → result (pickup, walk away) → the whole kiosk in context.

## Colour logic (brand palette mapped onto the style's roles)
- ground: warm paper `#F2EBDD` (mall floor), slab grid faint
- **subject hue = Cobalt Blue `#1F3BFF`**: the kiosk, counter front, fascia, staff tops
- **marking colour = Lime Zest `#D9F93A`**: the counter's light strip, logo ring, lead-in lines on the floor
- **accent = Tangerine `#FF6A2B`**: the rule only — the "stand here" outline, the arrow growing ahead of the customer, one accent character per floor phrase
- ink: Midnight Navy `#0B1026`; Sunshine `#FFC83D` for small props (food); kraft `#C9A06A` for bowls and bags
- customers' tops: ochre, sage, grey violet, oat (kept apart from cobalt and lime)

## Narration (Kokoro, calm and quick) → floor phrase
1. This is Bowlista: a whole food counter that fits in a box. → STORE IN A **BOX**
2. Walk up, and order at the screen. → **O**RDER
3. Pick your base: a bowl, a wrap, or a sub. → **B**ASE
4. Then add fresh fillings from the topping bar. → **F**RESH
5. Add a cold brew. And it's all one hundred percent veg. → **100%** VEG
6. Packed and placed on the shelf, for pickup or delivery. → **P**ICKUP
7. Bowlista. Fresh, and plant-powered. → PLANT-**POWERED**

## Cue map
~100 bpm. Soft vibraphone + upright piano (synthesised); a vibraphone note per landing floor character, the accent doubled on glockenspiel; pizzicato + shaker while the customer walks; a low sustained root across all beats; lid opening = paper flap + chime; resolve chord on the brand line.

## As built
25.5 s at 30 fps (shorter than planned: the seven narration lines run 18 s). Floor words were scaled to 0.56 so each lands fully inside the vertical frame. Walks use a steady pace (peak ≤ 2.6 m/s); the crowd motion check reports no jumps, overlaps or stepping. Delivery brands are not named or drawn.

## 5. The gap

1. **Small local organisers in Lucknow have no self-serve, vetted option.** The national players aim at big brands and run large managed contracts. Small organisers are left with WhatsApp groups and job boards (unvetted) or full-service planners (staff bundled in, not bookable on its own). Wedding planners, caterers, college fests and local shops running promotions sit in the empty quadrant. *Evidence: medium. Nobody found serves it; nobody proved it is empty.*
2. **Reliable, fast payment to workers.** The one complaint theme with repeated evidence is delayed or withheld pay. "Paid within 24 hours, guaranteed, even if the client pays late" would directly answer it and win the best workers. **This argues against charging ₹50–100 for same-day pay.** It's the thing workers most want, so make fast pay the default and the hook, or at least keep a free next-day option.
3. **Brand promotions is the crowded end.** OkayGo, Awign and Taskmo already supply promoters and sampling teams. Starting with weddings and local events avoids competing with them directly.

## 6. The threat: who could copy this fastest

- **Gigin** already lists Lucknow event jobs and has a hiring app. Adding ratings and self-serve booking for small organisers is a feature for them, not a new business.
- **OkayGo** and **Taskmo (Quess)** have the money and worker pools to add a small-client tier in any city once someone proves demand.
- **Lucknow planners** like Shri Events and Balaji already have staff lists. They could rent their crews to other organisers.

**The defence is local depth, not technology:** be the first with hundreds of rated Lucknow workers, relationships with planners and caterers, and a reputation for paying workers on time. A national player is unlikely to match that in one tier-2 city quickly.

# Eventify

- What it is: an app that matches companies and event organisers with paid, rated gig workers for events, weddings and promotions (the founder calls them "volunteers").
- Who it is for: two sides. (1) Companies, brands, event managers and wedding organisers in Lucknow who need short-term staff for marketing activations, leaflet distribution, event management and wedding support. (2) Students and part-time workers in Lucknow who want paid event work.
- What it sells, at what price:
  - Commission of about 10–20% on each payout. Not yet decided whether the company or the worker pays it.
  - Same-day payment fee for workers: ₹50–₹100 per payout.
  - Worker subscription that promises "guaranteed events". Price not set.
  - Hiring side: companies see each worker's past events, ratings and experience before hiring.
- Where and how: launching in Lucknow. A two-sided app or platform where companies post jobs and hire directly. Which type of event to start with (weddings, brand promotions or college fests) is not yet decided.
- Budget and constraints: not given (open question). The founder believes there is no organised platform for this today; that has not been checked.

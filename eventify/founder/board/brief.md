# Board brief: Eventify

(This brief stands on its own. The board has not spoken to the founder.)

## The idea, as the founder stated it
- **Name:** Eventify
- **Concept:** an organised platform for short-term event workers, whom the founder calls "volunteers", although they are paid. Companies can directly hire people for marketing, leaflet distribution, event management, and wedding/marriage event work.
- **The founder's premise:** "there is no organised platform for that" today. This has not been verified.
- **Hiring side (companies, brands, event managers, wedding organisers):** browse workers and see each one's past event experience and ratings, then hire them directly.
- **Worker side:** workers find paid event gigs.
- **Revenue, as proposed:**
  1. A commission of roughly 10–20% on each payout. Who pays it (the company or the worker) is not decided.
  2. A same-day payment fee of ₹50–₹100 per payout, paid by workers who want to be paid the same day.
  3. A worker subscription that promises "guaranteed events". No price has been set.
- **Launch city:** Lucknow, India.

## Not known yet (do not assume values)
- Which event type or customer to start with (weddings, brand promotions, college fests, corporate events).
- How many companies or organisers in Lucknow hire event staff, how often, and at what daily rates.
- How workers are found and paid today (agencies, WhatsApp groups, contractors) and what that costs.
- Who the existing competitors are (gig-staffing or part-time job apps), if any.
- Budget, team, tech plan, and the founder's experience in events or marketplaces.
- How "guaranteed events" would be honoured for subscribers.

# Laptop stand: 20 image prompts for a Meesho listing

## How to use

1. Open your image tool (ChatGPT, Gemini, or similar) and **upload your product photo as the reference** with every prompt.
2. Paste the **base block** first, then one numbered prompt.
3. Ask for a **square 1:1 image**, at least 1000 × 1000 px. Save each as JPG: `01.jpg` … `20.jpg`.
4. Check every result against the checklist at the end before you upload.

## Base block (paste before every prompt)

> Use the uploaded photo as the exact product reference. The product is a foldable aluminium laptop stand in matte silver: two parallel side rails joined by an X-shaped scissor brace with round silver hinge pins, slotted ventilation holes along the flat base bars, grey rubber pads on the upper arms, and small grey rubber hooks at the front lip that hold the laptop. Keep its shape, proportions, colour, hinges, slots and pads exactly as in the reference. Do not add or remove parts.
> Photorealistic product photography, soft natural light, sharp focus on the stand, square 1:1 image.
> Strict rules: no text, no words, no numbers, no watermark, no logo, no brand name anywhere, including on the laptop, screen, clothing, mugs, books or walls. Any laptop is a plain unbranded silver or grey laptop with no logo on the lid, and its screen shows a plain soft-colour wallpaper. No other brand's products recognisable. No collage, no borders, no added graphics or icons.

## The 20 prompts

### Product on white (main image and catalogue shots)
1. **Main image.** The stand fully opened at a medium height, three-quarter front view, alone on a pure white background (#FFFFFF), centred, filling about 85% of the frame, a soft natural shadow underneath.
2. **With laptop, white.** A plain unbranded silver laptop resting on the open stand at a comfortable typing angle, three-quarter side view, pure white background, the stand clearly visible under the laptop.
3. **Side profile.** The open stand seen exactly from the side, showing the X-brace and the slope angle, pure white background.
4. **Lowest and highest.** Two of the same stand side by side on a pure white background: the left one at its lowest setting, the right one at its highest, same lighting and scale.
5. **Folded flat.** The stand folded completely flat, lying at a slight angle, on a pure white background, showing how slim it becomes.
6. **Folded with pouch.** The folded stand lying next to its grey drawstring velvet pouch on a pure white background, the pouch slightly open.

### Close-up details
7. **Hinge close-up.** A macro shot of one round hinge pin and the scissor joint, shallow depth of field, soft grey studio background, showing the brushed aluminium finish.
8. **Rubber hooks close-up.** A macro shot of the front lip with its grey rubber hooks gripping the front edge of a plain grey laptop, soft studio background.
9. **Ventilation slots close-up.** A close-up looking down the base bar with its row of ventilation slots, soft side light raking across the metal, light grey background.
10. **Pads close-up.** A close-up of the grey rubber pads on the upper arms where the laptop rests, soft light grey background.

### People using it (lifestyle)
11. **Work from home.** An Indian woman in her late 20s in a casual kurta working on a plain unbranded laptop raised on the stand, at a wooden desk in a bright home room with a plant, shot from the side and slightly behind her shoulder so the stand is clearly visible, natural window light.
12. **Office desk.** An Indian man in his 30s in a plain light-blue shirt typing on a wireless keyboard, with his laptop raised to eye level on the stand, a tidy modern office desk, three-quarter view showing the stand and his upright posture.
13. **Student in a hostel.** A college student in a plain T-shirt studying at a small desk in a hostel room, laptop on the stand, notebooks beside it, warm evening lamp light, the stand clearly visible.
14. **Good posture.** Side view of a person sitting upright on a chair, the laptop screen raised to eye level by the stand on the desk, a clean neutral room, the comparison between posture and screen height obvious from the angle.
15. **Video call.** A young professional on a video call, the laptop raised on the stand so the camera is at eye level, a soft-focus bookshelf behind them (book spines plain, no readable titles), the stand in the foreground.
16. **Café.** A person working on a laptop on the stand at a small round café table, a coffee cup with no logo beside it, warm daylight, shallow depth of field, the stand sharp.

### Portability and setting
17. **Into the bag.** Hands sliding the folded stand inside its grey pouch into a plain backpack on a wooden table, close-up, the backpack with no logo.
18. **Flat-lay desk.** Top-down view of a minimal desk: the open stand holding a plain laptop, a wireless mouse, a notebook, a pen and a small plant, all unbranded, light wood surface.
19. **With a tablet.** The stand holding a plain unbranded tablet upright for watching, on a bedside table in a calm bedroom, soft lamp light.
20. **Cool and clear underneath.** Low angle from the side of a desk, showing the open space under the raised laptop where air flows, the stand's slotted base visible, soft light, clean grey wall behind.

## Check every image before uploading
- [ ] No text, numbers, watermark, logo or brand name anywhere (zoom in on the laptop lid, screen, mugs, clothes, books).
- [ ] The stand matches your real product: same rails, X-brace, hinge pins, slots, pads and silver colour. If the tool changed the design, regenerate. Buyers return products that don't match the photos.
- [ ] No extra legs, merged parts or warped hinges.
- [ ] The main image (01) is on pure white, the product fills most of the frame, and there's no person or props.
- [ ] Square, sharp, at least 1000 × 1000 px, saved as JPG.
- [ ] Faces and hands look natural. Discard any with extra fingers or distorted faces.

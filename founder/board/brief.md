# Board brief: Bowlista

(This brief stands on its own. The board has not spoken to the founder.)

## The idea, as the founder stated it
- **Name:** Bowlista
- **Concept:** 100% pure-veg ("100% PURE VEG" badge), plant-powered quick-service food: rice bowls, burritos/wraps, subs/sandwiches, and cold brew. Tagline: "Fresh. Plant-powered." Brand voice: Bold, Fresh, Fun. Signage reads "SUBS · BOWLS · WRAPS · COLD BREW"; social posts read "Bowls. Burritos. Subs. Brews."
- **Target customer:** college students at BBD (Babu Banarasi Das) University, Lucknow.
- **Location:** a single outlet in Crown Mall, near the BBD campus, Lucknow, India.
- **Channels:** dine-in / takeaway at the outlet, plus delivery on Swiggy and Zomato.
- **Pricing:** items "start from ₹69". The ₹69 items are a mini rice bowl, a mini burrito and a mini sandwich. Prices for full-size items and cold brew are not set.
- **Brand assets already done:** full brand board — logo (cobalt blue #1F3BFF and lime zest #D9F93A, plus off-white, midnight navy, tangerine, sunshine), bold rounded typography, patterns, icons, packaging mockups (bowl tub, foil burrito wrap with sleeve, sub box, cold brew cup, kraft bag), uniforms (cap, apron, tee), storefront signage, social templates.

## Not known yet (do not assume values)
- Setup budget, rent and fit-out cost at Crown Mall, whether the location is secured.
- Food cost per item, full menu prices, team/staffing.
- Footfall at Crown Mall, number of BBD students, competitor set near campus.
- The founder's experience in food service.
